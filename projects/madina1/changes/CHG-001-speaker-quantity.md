| BOQ | Item | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27.05 | Ceiling speaker Bosch LHM 0606/10, 6 W, 6 in, with back box | 6 no. | 1 no. |
| 27.08 | Speaker cable 2 x 1.5 mm2 shielded LSZH in EMT Ø25 | 13.9 m | about 4.5 m (single run, measured on site) |

Unchanged: 27.04 amplifier/mixer TOA A-2030 60 W in rack, 27.06 paging microphone (optional), 27.07 volume control, 27.09 Cat 6A.

## Why it is technically acceptable

The unit is 32.0 m2 of sales area (BOQ area line). Six 6 W speakers were a generic allowance, not a coverage calculation. One 6 W speaker tapped at 3 W, ceiling-mounted centrally, gives roughly 75-80 dBA across 32 m2 at 3 m, which is adequate for background music and cash-desk announcements in a shop of this size.

## Conditions attached to the change

1. Position it centrally over the sales floor, and **outside the 1500 mm design-control strip at the lease line**, where the BOQ forbids speakers.
2. Keep the amplifier: it also carries the microphone, USB/BT/FM source and the volume control.
3. If the mall or the civil defence authority requires voice alarm coverage in the unit, one speaker is not a PA/VA system and the requirement must be raised before the ceiling closes.

- ELEC model: reduce to one speaker, placed centrally, clear of the 1500 mm strip.
